Inner Joins in PostgreSQL

Three practice questions on the cars, car_companies and car_origins tables.

Look at your 3 tables in SupaBase: cars stores only two numbers where the maker and the country should be. Those numbers are foreign keys — pointers at rows in the other two tables. An INNER JOIN is how you turn them back into names, and it keeps only rows where both sides match: a car whose company_id pointed at a missing company would vanish from the result entirely, with no error.
