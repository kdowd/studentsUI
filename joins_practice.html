Inner Joins in PostgreSQL

Six practice questions on the makers, screens and smartphones tables.

The three tables

makers
  • maker_id  PK
  • maker_name
←— maker_id
←— screen_size_id
screens
  • screen_size_id  PK
  • size_inches
  • size_cms (generated)
smartphones
  • phone_id  PK
  • model_name
  • maker_id  FK → makers
  • screen_size_id  FK → screens
  • release_year
  • os
Why this matters. An INNER JOIN keeps only rows where both sides match. A phone whose screen_size_id has no matching row in screens would be dropped from the result entirely — silently, with no error. Every question below depends on those foreign keys pointing at real rows.
